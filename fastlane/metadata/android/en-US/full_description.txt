KillMyApps is a lightweight utility designed to terminate background processes, helping extend battery life, improve gaming performance, and free up system resources. It also helps reduce intrusive notifications, unwanted background activity, tracking and telemetry.

Important:
* The KILL feature requires Root or Shizuku access. Without it, you can still use the app, but you must manually use Android's Force Stop action for individual applications.
* You can choose between Root, Shizuku, and Shizuku+ as the Killer Engine from the app settings.

Features:
* ⚡ One-Tap Kill: Terminate running apps instantly with a single tap.
* 🔰 Killer Engine: Choose between Root, Shizuku, and Shizuku+ for app termination.
* 🎚️ Kill Levels: Choose between Soft, Deep and Killer levels.
* 🛡️ Source Mode: Categorize apps by User, Launchable, or System.
* 🔎 Selection Mode: Choose between Include and Exclude modes to control which apps are affected.
* ⏰ Auto Killer: Automatically terminate background apps when the screen turns off or at a scheduled daily time, with a customizable delay and missed-run fallback.
* 🫧 Quick Settings Tile: Kill running apps directly from Android Quick Settings.
* 🧩 Home Screen Widgets: Instantly terminate background processes from your home screen.
* 🔋 Save Battery: Reduce battery usage by stopping unnecessary background activity.
* 🚀 Boost Performance: Free up system resources for high-demand tasks like gaming.
* 🔒 Enhance Privacy: Reduce unnecessary background activity, tracking and telemetry.
* 🍃 Clean UI: Simple and intuitive Material Design interface.
